# 2. GOOGLE ADS IN 2026

## Campaign Types

### Performance Max
- Goal-based campaign type
- Runs across all Google properties (Search, Display, YouTube, Discover, Gmail)
- AI-driven bidding and placement optimization
- Requires: creative assets, budget, conversion signals
- Less transparent but more automated
- Best for: advertisers with strong conversion data

### Search Campaigns
- Text ads on SERPs
- Keyword targeting (broad, phrase, exact match)
- Responsive search ads (RSA) - 15 headlines, 4 descriptions
- Extensions: sitelink, callout, structured snippet, call, location
- Best for: high-intent capture

### Display Campaigns
- Visual ads across Google Display Network (2M+ sites)
- Targeting: audience, contextual, placement, retargeting
- Formats: image, responsive, video, native
- Best for: brand awareness, retargeting

### YouTube Ads
- TrueView in-stream (skippable after 5 seconds)
- TrueView discovery (appears alongside YouTube content)
- Bumper ads (6 seconds, unskippable)
- Masthead (top of YouTube homepage)
- Best for: video content, brand awareness, product demos

### Demand Gen Campaigns
- Visual-first campaigns across YouTube, Discover, Gmail
- Designed for AI-driven creative optimization
- Best for: driving consideration and conversions

## Bidding Strategies

### Automated Bidding
- **Target CPA**: Optimize for conversions at target cost-per-acquisition
- **Target ROAS**: Optimize for conversion value at target return
- **Maximize Conversions**: Get most conversions within budget
- **Maximize Conversion Value**: Get highest conversion value within budget
- **Target Impression Share**: Show at top/absolute top of SERP

### Manual Bidding
- Manual CPC: Full control over bids
- Enhanced CPC: Manual with automatic adjustments
- Best for: new campaigns without conversion data

## Structure Best Practices
- Organize by campaign goal (Sales, Leads, Brand Awareness)
- Use separate campaigns for brand vs non-brand
- Separate Search, Display, YouTube, Shopping campaigns
- Use ad groups for tightly themed keyword clusters
- 10-20 keywords per ad group (exact/phrase match focus)
- Negative keywords at campaign and ad group level

## Quality Score Factors
- Expected CTR
- Ad relevance (keyword-ad copy match)
- Landing page experience
- Historical account performance

## Google Ads Extensions
- Sitelink extensions (additional landing page links)
- Callout extensions (unique selling points)
- Structured snippet extensions (product categories)
- Call extensions (phone number)
- Location extensions (store address/map)
- Price extensions (product/service pricing)
- Promotion extensions (sale/offer details)

# 3. META ADS (FACEBOOK & INSTAGRAM)

## Campaign Structure

### Campaign Level
- Objective: Awareness, Traffic, Engagement, Leads, Sales
- Special ad categories (credit, employment, housing, social issues)

### Ad Set Level
- Targeting: demographics, interests, behaviors, custom audiences, lookalikes
- Placement: Feed, Stories, Reels, In-Stream, Search, Marketplace
- Budget: daily or lifetime
- Schedule: start/end dates, ad scheduling
- Optimization: delivery optimization, bid cap

### Ad Level
- Creative: image, video, carousel, collection, instant experience
- Primary text, headline, description
- CTA button
- Destination URL

## Advantage+ (AI-Powered)
- Advantage+ audience (broad targeting)
- Advantage+ creative (auto-generate creative variations)
- Advantage+ placements (spread budget across all placements)
- Advantage+ catalog ads (dynamic product ads)
- Advantage+ shopping campaigns

## Targeting Options

### Core Audiences
- Demographics: age, gender, location, language
- Interests: hobbies, interests, pages liked
- Behaviors: purchase behavior, device usage, travel

### Custom Audiences
- Website visitors (Meta pixel)
- Customer list (email/phone upload)
- App activity
- Engagement (video, lead form, shop, events)
- Offline activity

### Lookalike Audiences
- Based on custom audience source
- 1% (most similar) to 10% (broadest)
- Requires minimum 100 people from source

## Meta Pixel & Conversions API
- Pixel tracks website events
- Conversions API (CAPI) sends server-side events
- CAPI reduces reliance on pixel-based tracking
- Both improve ad targeting and measurement
- Enhanced matching for better attribution

## Ad Formats
- **Image**: Single image with text overlay (20% text rule relaxed but still preferred)
- **Video**: Full-motion video up to 240 minutes
- **Carousel**: 2-10 images/videos with links
- **Collection**: Cover image + product catalog
- **Instant Experience**: Full-screen mobile landing page
- **Stories**: Full-screen vertical (15 seconds)
- **Reels**: Short-form vertical video (up to 90 seconds)

# 4. TIKTOK ADS

## Campaign Types
- **In-Feed Ads**: Native video ads in For You feed
- **Spark Ads**: Boost organic posts from creators
- **TopView**: Full-screen ad on app open
- **Brand Takeover**: Splash screen on app open
- **Branded Hashtag Challenge**: Sponsored hashtag campaign
- **Branded Effects**: Custom filters and effects
- **TikTok Shop Ads**: Product-focused shopping ads

## Best Practices
- Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio
- 15-60 seconds optimal
- First 2-3 seconds must hook
- Use trending sounds and formats
- Authentic, raw content over polished production
- User-generated content style performs best
- Clear CTA with on-screen text
- Captions for accessibility and search

## TikTok Pixel & Events API
- Track website and app events
- Support for standard and custom events
- Event deduplication with other platforms
- Integration with TikTok Shop

## When TikTok Makes Sense
- Visually demonstrable products
- Discovery-oriented buying journeys
- Target audience uses TikTok as search/discovery
- Gen Z and younger millennial targets
- NOT ideal for: complex B2B offerings, high-consideration purchases with relationship-based trust

- **Sponsored Messaging**: InMail ads (message, conversation)

- Skills, groups, interests

# 6. PRIVACY-FIRST TARGETING STRATEGIES

## Post-Cookie Environment
- Third-party cookies phased out
- App tracking consent rates declined
- Apple ATT (App Tracking Transparency) limits iOS tracking
- Google's Privacy Sandbox for Chrome
- Email/phone-based targeting becomes primary

## First-Party Data Strategy
1. Collect zero-party data (preferences, intents via surveys/centers)
2. Capture first-party data (website behavior, purchase history)
3. Build CRM with enriched contact profiles
4. Use customer data platform (CDP) for unified profiles
5. Create custom audiences from first-party data
6. Use lookalike/actalike audiences from customer data

## Alternative Targeting Methods
- **Contextual targeting**: Place ads based on page content
- **Retargeting**: Reach users who visited your site
- **CRM matching**: Upload customer lists
- **Partner data**: Purchase from data providers
- **Platform algorithms**: Let AI optimize based on conversion signals
- **Retail media networks**: Amazon, Walmart, Instacart data

# 7. CROSS-CHANNEL ATTRIBUTION

## Attribution Models

### Single-Touch
- **First-click**: Credits first touchpoint
- **Last-click**: Credits final click (legacy, still common)

### Multi-Touch
- **Linear**: Equal credit to all touchpoints
- **Time decay**: More credit to recent touchpoints
- **Position-based**: 40% first/last, 20% middle
- **Data-driven**: Algorithm assigns credit (GA4, Google Ads)
- **Custom**: Build your own rules

## Measurement Guidelines
- Accept uncertainty in cross-channel attribution
- Use incrementality testing when possible
- Geo-lift experiments for true impact
- Media mix modeling (MMM) for macro-level
- Customer lifetime value (CLV) for full-funnel view

## Key Metrics by Funnel Stage
| Stage | Metric |
|-------|--------|
| Awareness | Impressions, Reach, CPM |
| Consideration | CTR, CPC, Engagement Rate |
| Conversion | CPA, ROAS, Conversion Rate |
| Retention | LTV, Repeat Purchase Rate, Churn |
| Full Funnel | Blended CPA, Total Revenue, MER |

# 8. BUDGET ALLOCATION STRATEGIES

## Framework by Business Stage

### Early Stage (0-5 customers)
- Focus: Brand awareness, demand generation
- Allocation: 60% awareness (Display, Social, Video), 30% consideration (Search), 10% conversion
- Goal: Validate messaging, find audience

### Growth Stage (5-100 customers)
- Focus: Customer acquisition, scaling
- Allocation: 30% awareness, 40% consideration, 30% conversion
- Goal: Scale proven channels, optimize CPA

### Mature Stage (100+ customers)
- Focus: Maximize LTV, efficiency
- Allocation: 20% awareness, 30% consideration, 50% conversion + retention
- Goal: Optimize ROAS, cross-sell, retain

## Budget Allocation by Channel

### Typical B2C Split
- Google Ads (Search + Shopping): 40-50%
- Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram): 25-35%
- TikTok Ads: 10-15%
- YouTube/Video: 5-10%
- Other (Pinterest, Snapchat, etc.): 5%

### Typical B2B Split
- Google Ads (Search): 30-40%
- LinkedIn Ads: 25-35%
- Meta Ads: 10-15%
- YouTube/Video: 10%
- Programmatic Display: 5-10%

## Budget Optimization Rules
1. Spend 70% on proven channels, 20% on testing, 10% on experimental
2. Scale winners quickly, cut losers ruthlessly
3. Use blended ROAS for overall efficiency
4. Set individual channel CPA targets
5. Reallocate monthly based on performance
6. Account for seasonality in planning

# 9. COMMON PPC MISTAKES

## Strategic Mistakes
1. No conversion tracking or poor setup
2. Broadcasting to broad audiences without segmentation
3. Ignoring negative keywords
4. Using exact match-only or broad match-only
5. Not separating brand and non-brand campaigns
6. Not testing ad copy and creative
7. Setting and forgetting campaigns
8. Budget allocation based on vanity metrics
9. Using last-click attribution exclusively
10. Not optimizing for mobile

## Execution Mistakes
- Too many keywords in one ad group
- Generic ad copy that matches nothing specific
- Landing pages that don't match ad promise
- No A/B testing
- Ignoring Quality Score
- Not using ad extensions
- Poor bid management
- Not segmenting by device/location/time
- Not tracking phone calls
- Ignoring search query reports
