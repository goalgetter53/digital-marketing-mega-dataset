## 1.1 AIDA FORMULA
**Attention -> Interest -> Desire -> Action**

TEMPLATE:
- **Attention:** [Bold statement/question about problem]
- **Interest:** [Intriguing detail that hooks curiosity]
- **Desire:** [Benefit so good they want it]
- **Action:** [Clear command to click/buy/subscribe]

EXAMPLES:
- "Stop Wasting Money on Ads That Don't Work. Here's the 3-Step System That Cut Our CPA by 64% in 14 Days. Click Here."
- "Tired of Cold Emails Getting Ignored? Discover the 1-Line Subject Line That Got Us 47% Open Rates. Steal It Below."
- "Want Flat Abs Without the Gym? Research Shows This 7-Minute Morning Routine Burns 2x More Fat. Try It Free."

## 1.2 PAS FORMULA (Problem-Agitate-Solve)
*From Copyblogger's Demian Farnworth*

TEMPLATE:
- **Problem:** [Identify the pain/difficulty]
- **Agitate:** [Make it hurt â€” describe the consequences of NOT solving it]
- **Solve:** [Present your solution]

EXAMPLES:
- "Insecure? You're not alone. Millions of people admit to being insecure. Yet, remain that way and you'll live a life in the shadows. A life on the fringe. Always wishing, never doing. Fortunately, there's an answer."
- "Tired of girls not looking at you? You're not alone. There are thousands of guys trying everything to get women to pay attention to them. But at the end of the day, you just want a nice woman to hold you. That will never happen until she thinks you are a man. Like learn how to tame a grizzly bear. With your bare hands. Check out this video."
- "Frustrated with your job? Hate your boss? Love to work for yourself? You're not alone. Every day you stay, a piece of your soul dies. The commute, the meetings, the politics. There IS a way out."

## 1.3 BAB FORMULA (Before-After-Bridge)

TEMPLATE:
- **Before:** [Describe the world before â€” the pain, the struggle]
- **After:** [Paint the world after â€” the transformation, the dream]
- **Bridge:** [Your product/service is the bridge]

EXAMPLES:
- "Before: I was broke, buried in debt, and working 80-hour weeks. After: I now work 4 hours a week from a beach in Bali, making $20k/month passive income. The Bridge: This 1-page business blueprint."
- "Before: My skin was a disaster â€” acne, redness, uneven tone. After: Now people ask me what foundation I'm wearing (I'm not wearing any). The Bridge: This 3-step Korean skincare system."
- "Before: Couldn't get a single client. After: Turned away 12 clients last month. The Bridge: My cold email script that gets 40% reply rates."

## 1.4 FAB FORMULA (Features-Advantages-Benefits)

TEMPLATE:
- **Feature:** [What it is]
- **Advantage:** [What it does]
- **Benefit:** [What it means for THEM]

EXAMPLES:
- "Feature: Our mattress has 7-zone support. Advantage: It distributes weight evenly across your body. Benefit: You wake up pain-free and energized every single morning."
- "Feature: 100% organic cotton. Advantage: No synthetic chemicals touch your skin. Benefit: No more unexplained rashes for your baby."
- "Feature: AI-powered content analysis. Advantage: It scans 10,000+ articles in seconds. Benefit: You instantly know which topics will rank #1."

## 1.5 4-PS FORMULA (Picture-Promise-Prove-Push)

TEMPLATE:
- **Picture:** [Paint a vivid mental image of the result]
- **Promise:** [Make a specific commitment]
- **Prove:** [Show evidence, testimonials, data]
- **Push:** [Call to action]

EXAMPLES:
- "Picture yourself waking up every morning excited to check your bank account. Now imagine having $5,000 extra every month. I promise you can achieve this in 90 days. Over 2,000 students have done it. Click here to join them."
- "Picture your phone buzzing non-stop with new leads. I promise you'll get 50+ leads in your first week with this system. Our clients average 87 leads per campaign. Start your free trial."

## 1.6 1-2-3-4 FORMULA (Copyblogger)

TEMPLATE:
1. [Identify the problem in one sentence]
2. [Expand on why it matters]
3. [Present your unique solution]
4. [Tell them what to do next]

EXAMPLE:
"1. Most businesses waste 80% of their ad budget. 2. That's $80 of every $100 down the drain â€” money you could reinvest into growth. 3. Our AI optimization tool reallocates spend in real-time to what's actually converting. 4. Connect your ad account and see the difference in 24 hours."

## 1.7 SLAP FORMULA (Stop-Look-Act-Purchase)

TEMPLATE:
- **Stop:** [Stop them with a shocking statement/question]
- **Look:** [Make them look closer with curiosity]
- **Act:** [Trigger action with urgency/scarcity]
- **Purchase:** [Ask for the sale]

EXAMPLE:
"Stop: 90% of new businesses fail in Year 1. Look: But here's what the 10% do differently â€” and it's not what you think. Act: Grab our free blueprint before 500 downloads. Purchase: Get the full system here."

TEMPLATE:

- **Conviction:** [Build trust and desire]
- **Action:** [Compel them to act]
