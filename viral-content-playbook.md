## PART 1: CAROUSEL DESIGN & STRUCTURE

---

### 1.1 PERFORMANCE DATA

- Carousels avg engagement rate: ~10%
- Single-image posts: ~7%
- Reels: ~6%
- Up to 20 images/videos per carousel
- Carousel ads = lower CPC + higher CTR vs single-image

### 1.2 CAROUSEL SIZE SPECS

- Landscape: 1080 x 566 px (1.91:1)
- Portrait: 1080 x 1350 px (4:5) BEST
- Square: 1080 x 1080 px (1:1)
- Width always 1080px
- Video length: 3-60 sec, .MP4/.MOV
- Max video size: 4GB

### 1.3 CAROUSEL SLIDE-BY-SLIDE FRAMEWORKS

#### A: Educational/Listicle (9 slides)

Slide 1: HOOK - Bold statement + curiosity gap. "5 things no one tells you about [topic]". Big bold text, minimal imagery, high contrast.
Slide 2: INTRO - Why this matters. "Here's why most people get this wrong..." Problem visualization.
Slide 3-7: POINTS 1-5 - Each = one insight. "[Number] - [One-line punch]". Visual + short text.
Slide 8: BONUS - Extra tip or summary.
Slide 9: CTA - Save/tag/share. Clear brand colors.

#### B: Storytelling (8 slides)

Slide 1: HOOK - "I tried [method] for 30 days. Here's what happened." Teaser.
Slide 2: SETUP - Starting point, relatable struggle.
Slide 3: PROCESS - What they did.
Slide 4: STRUGGLE - Obstacle, emotional low.
Slide 5: BREAKTHROUGH - Key discovery.
Slide 6: RESULT - Outcome with data.
Slide 7: KEY TAKEAWAY - Single actionable insight.
Slide 8: CTA - Comment prompt.

#### C: Before/After (7 slides)

Slide 1: HOOK - Tease transformation.
Slide 2: BEFORE - Problem state.
Slide 3: THE CHANGE - What caused it.
Slide 4: AFTER - The result.
Slide 5: HOW IT WORKS - Brief steps.
Slide 6: WHY IT MATTERS - Deeper benefit.
Slide 7: CTA - Save/try.

#### D: Product Showcase (8 slides)

Slide 1: HOOK - "Our top picks for [category]". Product collage.
Slide 2-6: Individual products - image + 2-3 features + price.
Slide 7: COMPARISON - Quick chart.
Slide 8: CTA - Shop/collection link.

#### E: Step-by-Step Tutorial (9 slides)

Slide 1: HOOK - "How to [result] in [number] steps". Final result tease.
Slide 2-6: Steps 1-5 - Number + verb + description + visual.
Slide 7: COMMON MISTAKE - What to avoid.
Slide 8: RESULT - Completed outcome reveal.
Slide 9: CTA - Save/share.

#### F: Quiz/Interactive (9 slides)

Slide 1: HOOK - "Answer [number] questions to find out [result]". Question mark visual.
Slide 2-6: Questions with A/B/C/D options.
Slide 7: REVEAL - "Here's what your answers mean..."
Slide 8: DEEPER INSIGHT - Explanation per type.
Slide 9: CTA - Tag a friend.

#### G: Myth vs Fact (9 slides)

Slide 1: HOOK - "Stop believing these [number] [topic] myths". Bold debunking.
Slide 2-7: Myth/fact pairs. "Myth: [belief]" / "Fact: [truth]" + checkmark.
Slide 8: SUMMARY - Quick visual recap.
Slide 9: CTA - Save cheat sheet.

#### H: List/Curation (9 slides)

Slide 1: HOOK - "[Number] [category] you need to know". Number-focused design.
Slide 2-7: Entries 1-6 - Name + why it matters.
Slide 8: BONUS - Honorable mention.
Slide 9: CTA - Comment favorite.

**Page 1 Visual Rules:**

- High contrast background
- Single focal point, NO clutter
- Brand colors subtle

**Pages 3-7 Body Rules:**
- 1 idea per slide
- 3-7 words max main text
- Supporting visual
- Consistent layout
- Progressive reveal

**Page 9 CTA Types:**
- Save: "Save this for your next [project]"
- Comment: "Which tip will you try first?"
- Share: "Tag someone who needs this"
- Follow: "Follow @[handle] for more"
- Link: "Link in bio for [resource]"

### 1.5 CAROUSEL DESIGN BEST PRACTICES

**Typography:** Max 2 fonts. Sans-serif (Inter, Montserrat, Helvetica). Headline 48-72pt, Body 24-36pt. High contrast. No thin weights on dark.

**Color:** 2-3 brand colors max. One accent for CTA. Consistent. High contrast. White space.

**Imagery:** Consistent style. Same color grade. No watermarks. People > text-only. Product on consistent bg.

**Layout:** Same margins. Consistent text placement. Hierarchy: Hook > Image > Supporting. Leave room for Instagram UI.

**Canva:** 1080x1350 custom size. Lock grids. Master slides. Brand kit. Export PNG. Consistent page numbers.

### 2.7 VIRAL VIDEO FORMULA: HOOK-RETENTION-PAYOFF

HOOK (0-3 sec) > CURIOSITY GAP (3-7 sec) > VALUE DELIVERY (7-70%) > RETENTION MOMENT (mid-video pattern interrupt) > PAYOFF (last 20%) > CTA.

**Retention Techniques:**
- "But here's the thing..." (pattern interrupt)
- "Wait, there's more..."
- "This is where it gets interesting..."
- Visual change (new location, B-roll)
- Audio shift (beat drop, silence)
- Speed change (slow motion)
- "And then this happened..."

**Pacing Rules:**
- Change frame every 2-3 seconds
- Audio: 3+ distinct moments (intro, build, peak)
- Text on audio beats
- No static shots > 5 sec
- New info every 7-10 sec

### 2.8 EDITING PATTERNS & TRANSITIONS

**10 Popular Transitions:**
1. Match Cut (same shape/color)
2. Swipe/Wipe (hand across camera)
3. Clothes Change (outfit swap)
4. Camera Angle Change
5. Zoom Transition (rapid in/out)
6. Rotate (camera flip)
7. Object Cover (object passes camera)
8. Stop Motion (freeze frame)
9. Light Change (flash to black/white)
10. Walk Through (walk toward camera)

**On-Beat Editing:** Major visual changes + text appearances + transitions all land on audio beats. Speed ramps match music energy.

**Text Overlay Rules:**
- 30%+ watch sound-off
- Center captions
- Bold keywords
- 2-3 words per block
- Sync with speech
- Highlight bg for readability
- Max 3 lines
- Bold sans-serif 24-48pt min
- Top: headline. Middle: content. Bottom: captions.
- NEVER cover face > 1 sec

**Pacing by Length:**
0-15 sec: 5-8 cuts, very high density
15-30 sec: 8-15 cuts, high density
30-60 sec: 15-25 cuts, medium
60-90 sec: 20-35 cuts, medium-low
90-180 sec: 30-50 cuts, narrative

## PART 3: VIDEO PRODUCTION

---

### 3.1 EQUIPMENT

Minimum: Smartphone + natural light + tripod.
Audio clarity > video quality. Voiceover recorded separately. -6dB to -3dB peak.
Lighting: Face brightest element, no harsh shadows, window light soft, ring light at eye level 45 degrees.

### 3.2 SHOOTING

Camera at eye level. Look at lens. Stable shots. Clean bg. Good audio. Natural expressions. Headroom: 1/3 screen for face. Record 4K, export 1080p. Multiple takes.

Faceless: B-roll compilations, screen recordings + voiceover, text-on-screen animations, stock footage overlays, animated infographics.

### 3.3 CAPTION FORMULAS

Formula 1: Hook + Context + CTA
Formula 2: Value First
Formula 3: Story + Lesson

Limits: Reels 2,200 chars. TikTok 4,000 chars (first 100-150 visible).

### 4.1 70-20-10 MIX

70% Value/Educational. 20% Engagement/Brand (BTS, trends). 10% Promotional.

### 4.2 CONTENT CYLINDERS

1. TRENDING: 3-7 day life, 30 min production
2. EVERGREEN: Months/years life, higher production
3. ENGAGEMENT: Community building, interactive
4. STORYTELLING: Brand narrative, emotional

### 4.3 POSTING CADENCE

Minimum: 3-5x/week Reels, 2-3x/week Carousels, 1-2x/day Stories.
Growth: 1-2x/day Reels, 3-5x/week Carousels.
One 10/10 > five 5/10. Batch create.

### 4.4 CROSS-PLATFORM REPURPOSING

Carousel > Reel: Screen record swipe + trending audio + voiceover.
Reel > Shorts/TikTok: Remove watermarks, re-edit pacing, change captions/hashtags/keywords.
Blog > Carousel: 5-10 key points, one per slide, visual each, title + CTA slides, link to full.

### 4.5 KEY METRICS

Reels: watch time, completion, replays, shares, saves, comments, profile visits, follows.
TikTok: watch time, completion, replays, shares, FYP %.
Shorts: views, retention, engagement, traffic sources, subscriber growth.
