# 1. INSTAGRAM ALGORITHM 2026
====================================================================================================

## CORE CONCEPT
Instagram uses MULTIPLE algorithms (Feed, Reels, Stories, Explore) each with different ranking signals. No single algorithm governs the entire app.

## GLOBAL GOAL: Maximize time spent on app by delivering relevant, engaging content.

## TOP RANKING SIGNAL 2026: SHARES (Sends Per Reach)
- Confirmed by Adam Mosseri (Instagram Head) as #1 signal across ALL surfaces
- Private sharing (DMs) is the strongest indicator of value
- Content sent to friends ? algorithm treats as highly distributable to new audiences

## SECONDARY CORE SIGNALS (Across All Surfaces):
- Views (primary currency metric — unified across all formats)
- Originality (Aggregator Penalty — see below)
- Watch time / Dwell time
- Saves ? strong signal (high-value content worth revisiting)
- Comments ? meaningful interaction
- Likes ? baseline signal (weakest of engagement signals)
- Profile visits ? indicates interest in creator

## INSTAGRAM FEED ALGORITHM
### Ranking Signals:
1. Your activity — topics/accounts you already like, save, send
2. Info about the post — virality, engagement rate, recency
3. Info about the poster — similarity to accounts user already follows
4. History of interaction — how often user engages with that specific creator
5. Negative signals — how often users skip, scroll past quickly, bounce after few seconds
6. Content type preference — Reels vs photos vs carousels
7. Recency — newer posts show first
8. Safety — content breaking guidelines gets suppressed/removed

### Feed Predictions (what algorithm predicts for ranking):
- Likelihood to spend 10+ seconds on post
- Likelihood to click creator's profile
- Likelihood to comment
- Likelihood to reshare/send

### Feed Limits:
- Algorithm limits back-to-back posts from same creator
- Blends followed + recommended accounts

## INSTAGRAM REELS ALGORITHM
### Ranking Signals:
1. Your activity in Reels — liked, saved, shared, commented on
2. Info about the reel — audio, video quality, popularity signals
3. Info about the poster — engagement history, relationship to you
4. History of interaction — past engagement with that creator
5. Watch time / retention — how long people watch
6. Rewatches — whether viewers rewatch
7. Speed of engagement — how quickly people interact
8. Follow-through — whether viewers follow creator after watching

### Reels Predictions:
- Likelihood to watch to end
- Likelihood to tap into audio page
- Likelihood to rewatch or reshare
- Likelihood to follow creator

### What KILLS Reels Distribution (Penalties):
- WATERMARKED CONTENT — visible TikTok/other platform watermarks (DEPRIORITIZED)
- LOW-QUALITY/BLURRY VIDEO — algorithm favors crisp, well-produced
- RECYCLED CONTENT — reposting same Reel multiple times
- ENGAGEMENT BAIT — "like for part 2" / "comment YES" triggers penalties
- MISLEADING CONTENT — clickbait that doesn't deliver
- NO AUDIO — Reels without audio
- TOO MUCH TEXT — visually cluttered
- POLITICAL CONTENT — may be restricted
- COMMUNITY GUIDELINES VIOLATIONS

### Reels Best Practices (Adam Mosseri):
- Strong hook in first 3 seconds
- Turn on captions
- Include audio
- Post non-watermarked, original content
- Keep Reels 3 minutes or less
- Check Account Status (must be in good standing)

## INSTAGRAM STORIES ALGORITHM
### Ranking Signals:
1. Viewing history — accounts whose Stories you regularly watch
2. Engagement history — accounts you've DMed or reacted to
3. Closeness — how likely it's a close friend/family (DM frequency, Facebook connection)
4. Recency — newer Stories prioritized
5. Interaction frequency — likes, replies, emoji reactions

### Stories Predictions (top factors per Mosseri):
- Likelihood to tap into Story
- Likelihood to like it
- Likelihood to reply to it

### Stories Best Practices:
- Post consistently (creators who post Stories often see fewer unfollows)
- Don't post too many at once (views/engagement dip after 5 slides)
- Use interactive stickers (polls, questions, quizzes, emoji sliders)
- Include sticker on FIRST story for added boost
- Use link stickers for traffic

## INSTAGRAM EXPLORE PAGE ALGORITHM
- Explore = entirely content from NEW accounts (not followed)
- Has 36 ranking signals (most of any surface) per Meta Transparency Center

### Top Explore Signals:
1. Your likelihood of FOLLOWING the creator
2. Your likelihood of viewing post for 5+ seconds
3. Your likelihood of finishing video (95%+)
4. Activity in Explore — how you've interacted with posts historically
5. Info about the post — popularity, how quickly users interacting
6. Info about the poster — how many interactions in past few weeks
7. Speed of engagement — rapid likes/comments/shares/saves

## INSTAGRAM SEO SIGNALS:
- Keywords in captions ? searchable
- Keywords in bio ? helps account discovery
- Alt text with keywords ? can appear in Explore
- Hashtags (quality over quantity — 3-5 specific is recommended)
- Subtitles/captions on Reels ? improves discoverability
- Location tagging

## 2026 UPDATES:
1. **Shares are TOP signal** — confirmed by Mosseri
2. **Trial Reels** — show to non-followers first; if engagement strong, push to followers; can delete without followers seeing
3. **Longer Reels eligible for Explore** — up to 3 minutes
4. **AI Translations** — auto-translates Reels captions/audio (Hindi, Portuguese, English, Spanish + more)
5. **Carousels support up to 20 slides** (was 10)
6. **Reset Suggested Content** — Settings > Content Preferences > Reset; wipes recommendation history
7. **Your Algorithm feature** (Dec 2025) — users review topics IG thinks they care about, add new ones, down-rank unwanted
8. **Teen Restriction Accounts** — auto protective settings
9. **Views as primary metric** — unified across all formats
10. **Aggregator Penalty** — reposting without adding value ? algorithm replaces with original creator's version

## METRICS TO TRACK:
| Metric | What It Indicates |
|--------|-------------------|
| Reach rate | % of followers who see content |
| Share rate | How often content sent to others (#1 signal) |
| Save rate | How often bookmarked |
| Profile visits (non-followers) | Discovery beyond existing audience |
| Follower growth rate | Sustained algorithmic favor |
| Watch time | Retention quality |
| Sends per reach | Primary distribution driver |

## HOW TO RESET YOUR INSTAGRAM ALGORITHM:
Settings > Content Preferences > Reset suggested content

# 2. TIKTOK ALGORITHM 2026
====================================================================================================

## CORE CONCEPT
Recommendation system for For You Page (FYP). Highly personalized — no two users see same FYP.

## THREE SIGNAL CATEGORIES (with weights):

### SIGNAL TYPE: USER INTERACTIONS — WEIGHT: HIGH (MOST IMPORTANT)
- Videos liked/shared
- Videos added to favorites
- Accounts followed
- Comments posted
- Videos marked "Not Interested" (negative signal)
- Videos reported (negative signal)
- Watch time (STRONGEST SINGLE SIGNAL)
- Completion rate (STRONGEST SINGLE SIGNAL)
- Replays (signals high engagement)
- Content you create yourself
- Interest expressed via organic content AND ads

### SIGNAL TYPE: VIDEO INFORMATION — WEIGHT: MEDIUM
- Captions
- Sounds/audio
- Hashtags
- Effects
- Trending topics
- Video length
- ON-SCREEN TEXT (algorithm reads it)
- SPOKEN WORDS (algorithm analyzes via auto-captions)
- SEARCH VALUE (keyword matching to user queries)

### SIGNAL TYPE: USER INFORMATION — WEIGHT: LOW (least impact)
- Language preference
- Country setting
- Device type
- Category selection (new user preferences)
- Time zone

## SIGNAL STRENGTH HIERARCHY:
- STRONGEST: Watch time, completion rate, replays, shares
- STRONG: Comments, follows from video, saves/favorites
- MODERATE: Likes, hashtag engagement
- WEAKEST: Device type, language, location

## RANKING FACTORS DETAILED:
1. Engagement — saves & shares (weighted highest in 2026), likes, comments, completion rate, favorites
2. User Interactions — accounts followed, content created, topic authority
3. Video Information — captions, hashtags, sounds, search value (spoken words via auto-captions + text overlays)
4. Device & Account — country, language, device type, category (least weight)

## WHAT TIKTOK ALGORITHM DOES NOT SHOW:
- Duplicate content
- Content already seen
- Spam
- Potentially offensive/unsafe content
- Videos just uploaded (under review)
- Content from accounts under 16 years old

## WHAT TIKTOK ALGORITHM DOES NOT CONSIDER:
- Follower count (new creators have equal chance)
- Previous high-performing videos

## CONTENT INELIGIBLE FOR FYP RECOMMENDATION:
1. Content by anyone under 16
2. Hate speech/hateful behavior
3. Disordered eating/body image
4. Dangerous activity/challenges
5. Nudity/body exposure
6. Sexually suggestive content
7. Shocking/graphic content
8. Misinformation
9. False/unverified civic/election claims
10. Fake engagement ("like-for-like")
11. Unoriginal content (watermarked, simple GIFs)
12. Gambling
13. Promoting tobacco/drugs/excessive alcohol
14. Live content driving people off-platform

## 2026 CHANGES:
1. Search as bigger discovery channel — 84% of TikTok searches in exploration phase; keyword-optimized captions/on-screen text critical
2. Longer videos rewarded — 1-3 minutes+ with high completion rates get distribution
3. TikTok Shop signals — commerce-tagged content can appear in shopping feeds
4. Content diversity priority — TikTok avoids showing too much repetitive content
5. STEM feed expanded — educational/science content discovery surface
6. Photo Mode (carousels) — unique algorithm based on Swipe-Through Rate (STR)
7. Search Value as direct ranking metric — algorithm "listens" to spoken/text content for query matching
8. Qualified Views — 5+ second views now the quality threshold

## BEST PRACTICES:
- Hook in first 3 seconds (or lose viewer)
- Videos 60-90 seconds often see higher reach (Creator Rewards Program)
- Keyword-rich captions + voiceover + text overlays
- Trending sounds, hashtags, effects
- Post when audience active (general: Tue-Thu 2-6pm)
- Consistency 3-4x/week (topic authority > posting frequency)
- Native content made FOR TikTok (79% of users prefer brands that understand TikTok-specific content)
- Edutainment formula (education + entertainment) = most consistent path to virality

## PENALTIES:
- Deleting videos ? removes algorithm data points; mass-delete triggers spam filters
- Low completion rate ? "200-view jail"
- Engagement bait ? suppression
- Reposted/watermarked content ? deprioritized

# 3. YOUTUBE ALGORITHM 2026
====================================================================================================

## CORE CONCEPT
Recommendation system drives 70% of what people watch. The algorithm doesn't evaluate content itself — it measures how viewers interact with it.

## FIVE RANKING FACTOR CATEGORIES:

### 1. VIEWER ENGAGEMENT SIGNALS
- CTR (Click-Through Rate) — how often users click on thumbnail
- Watch time & retention — total minutes watched, avg % viewed
- Likes, comments, shares — strong audience interest
- NEGATIVE FEEDBACK — "Not Interested" / dislikes ? suppresses recommendations

### 2. VIEWER SATISFACTION
- Satisfaction surveys (millions of responses collected)
- Post-watch behavior — does viewer continue watching or leave?
- Replays & return visitors — high satisfaction signal

### 3. PERSONALIZED RECOMMENDATIONS (User Behavior + Context)
- Watch history
- Viewing habits (short-form morning vs long-form night)
- Device type (mobile vs TV ? different content)
- Time of day

### 4. LONG-TERM RELEVANCE
- Search trends ? can boost older videos when topic resurfaces
- Evergreen content can resurface months/years later

### 5. MULTI-LANGUAGE SIGNALS
- YouTube tracks video performance SEPARATELY for each language
- Dubbing content ? increased international reach
- Channels dubbing 80%+ catalog perform better globally

## YOUTUBE SHORTS ALGORITHM
### Shorts Ranking Factors:
1. Engagement — likes, comments, shares
2. Watch duration — closer to 100% the better
3. Replays/loops — naturally looping Shorts get more recommendations
4. Content variety — avoids too many from same creator back-to-back
5. User preferences — tailored based on likes, watches, skips
6. Viewed-vs-swiped-away ratio — #1 metric
7. Search relevance (SEO) — with 2026 Shorts filter in Search, titles/descriptions now critical
8. Thumbnails — critical for search results, Related tab, channel pages
9. Freshness/frequency — recent updates favor fresh content
10. CTR is NOT a ranking factor (users don't click Shorts — they swipe)

### Shorts DON'Ts:
- Reposting with watermarks (TikTok/IG logos) ? algorithm downranks immediately
- Inconsistent formatting (horizontal videos cropped poorly) ? detected and suppressed

## 2026 CHANGES:
1. Hyper-personalization with AI — recognizes time-of-day + device patterns
2. Hype feature — creators with 500-500k subscribers; fans can "Hype" new video ? temporary ranking boost
3. Older videos get new life — algorithm better at recognizing evergreen relevance
4. AI labeling required — synthetic content must be labeled; failure ? algorithmic suppression
5. Multi-language performance tracking — each language version = own performance track
6. Shorts search filters — users can filter search specifically for Shorts ? SEO now critical

# 4. LINKEDIN ALGORITHM 2026
====================================================================================================

## CORE CONCEPT
LinkedIn is DESIGNED TO PREVENT VIRALITY. About sharing knowledge with people who care.

## THREE-STEP FILTERING PROCESS:

### STEP 1: QUALITY FILTERING
Initial classification: spam, low-quality, or high-quality.

#### Spammy behavior violations:
- Tagging unrelated individuals
- Low-quality content (errors flagged)
- Excessive tags (keep 3-5 per post)
- Too frequent posting (minimum 12 hours between posts)
- Emoji/reaction polls for artificial engagement
- Chain-letter content requesting likes/reactions/shares

### STEP 2: ENGAGEMENT TESTING ("Golden Hour")
- Post shown to small sample of audience
- If strong engagement in first hour ? pushed to 2nd and 3rd degree connections
- Meaningful engagement valued over generic interaction

### STEP 3: NETWORK + RELEVANCE RANKING
#### Three ranking signals:
1. IDENTITY — location, career, skills
2. CONTENT — dwell time, views, topic relevance, topic authority of poster
3. MEMBER ACTIVITY — past actions, hashtags followed, connections

## 2025/2026 UPDATES:
1. Improved visibility for experts
2. Moved away from clickbait detection
3. Rewarding high-engagement posts
4. Native content gets boost (put links in comments)
5. Prioritizing relevance over recency — older posts (2-3 weeks old) shown if more relevant

## CONTENT TYPES THAT PERFORM BEST:
- Conversational content (questions, personal stories)
- Video (5x engagement; Live 24x)
- Carousels/documents (highest reach format)
- Value-driven posts (actionable tips)
- Question-based posts
- Visual content (infographics, quotes, memes)
- Employee-centric content
- Bite-sized advice

## BEST PRACTICES:
- First 3 lines = hook
- 3-5 hashtags per post
- Post weekly minimum (2x lift)
- Use native features (polls, documents, video, live)
- Tag relevant people (max 5)
- Employee advocacy
- Respond to comments
- Dwell time optimization

# 5. X / TWITTER ALGORITHM 2026
====================================================================================================

## CORE CONCEPT
"Pay-for-reach" model. Grok AI integrated. Open-source code confirmed interaction weights.

## RANKING SIGNALS (with confirmed weights):

### 1. RECENCY
- Steep "time decay" — post loses HALF visibility score every 6 hours

### 2. ENGAGEMENT (with confirmed multipliers):
- Reposts (Retweets): ~20x value of a Like
- Replies: ~13.5x value of a Like
- Bookmarks: ~10x value of a Like
- Likes: 1x (baseline)

### 3. ACCOUNT CREDIBILITY & X PREMIUM
- X Premium subscribers get 2x to 4x boost in reach vs non-Premium
- Premium user replies prioritized at top of threads
- Verification = credibility signal

### 4. TYPE OF CONTENT
- Rich media favored (GIFs, images, videos, polls)
- PENALTY for external links: 50-90% reach reduction
- Text-only posts and X Articles perform better than outbound links

### 5. RELEVANCY
- Grok AI determines "SimClusters" — communities discussing similar topics
- AI analyzes SEMANTIC MEANING of post (not just keywords)

## PENALTIES:
- External links ? 50-90% reach reduction
- Spam detection ? suppression
- Engagement bait ? filtering

## 2026 CHANGES:
1. Pay-centric algorithm — organic reach for non-Premium accounts significantly lower
2. Grok AI integration — semantic understanding, SimClusters
3. X "Article" feature — long-form content directly on platform (algorithm rewards)
4. Video-first push — native video prioritized over YouTube links

## BEST PRACTICES:
- Post consistently throughout day
- Rich media content
- Put external LINKS IN FIRST REPLY
- Use X "Article" feature for long-form
- Prioritize native video
- Respond quickly (within 2-3 hours)
- Consider X Premium for organic growth

## INTERACTION MULTIPLIERS SUMMARY:
| Action | Value vs Like |
|--------|---------------|
| Repost | ~20x |
| Reply | ~13.5x |
| Bookmark | ~10x |
| Like | 1x (baseline) |
